# Demas Group — Homepage Content & Layout Brief

**Prepared for:** Custom WordPress/WooCommerce theme (hand-built, native blocks)
**Scope:** Homepage only. No code — content, copy, and composition direction.
**Audience:** Commercial and institutional buyers in Saudi Arabia (municipalities, contractors, developers, facilities operators). Not residential.

---

## Guiding idea

The homepage should read like the company itself: engineering-led, quietly confident, and built on proof. Every section answers one buyer question in sequence — *Who are you?* → *Can I trust you?* → *What do you supply?* → *How do you work?* → *How do I start?* Nothing decorative that doesn't carry information. The visual language is premium infrastructure, not garden lifestyle: navy and white doing the structural work, gold used sparingly as a highlight (certifications, key numbers, primary CTA), serif headlines over sans-serif body to signal heritage backed by precision.

**Resolved:** experience claim confirmed at **45 years** — used consistently below.

---

## Section 1 — Hero: "From the Ground Up"

**What it is:** Full-viewport cinematic hero built on the locked 4-frame photography sequence (underground infrastructure → intelligent control → precision irrigation running → thriving finished landscape).

**Why it's there:** First impression must immediately reposition the brand from "equipment vendor" to "engineering partner." The 4-frame narrative does this visually — it shows the *system*, not the product. The buyer should feel: these people understand the whole lifecycle.

**Draft headline:**
> **Water, engineered.**

**Supporting copy:**
> From buried pipelines to intelligent control systems, Demas Group designs, supplies, and supports complete irrigation and landscape infrastructure across the Kingdom. Riyadh-based. ISO-certified. Trusted on projects where water can't be left to chance.

**CTAs:** Primary — *Explore the Catalog* (gold button). Secondary — *Talk to an Engineer* (outlined, white/light blue).

**Layout/composition:** The four frames play as a slow auto-advancing sequence (roughly 4–5 seconds per frame, gentle crossfade — no hard cuts, no carousel arrows). Each frame carries a small numbered caption in the lower corner ("01 — Beneath the surface", "02 — Intelligent control", "03 — Precision in motion", "04 — The result") so the story is legible even at a glance. Headline and CTAs sit lower-left over a subtle navy gradient scrim to guarantee contrast against golden-hour photography. Keep the headline static while frames change behind it — the fixed text against moving imagery reinforces "one company, whole system." On mobile, the sequence still auto-advances but captions move below the image to preserve legibility.

## Section 2 — Credentials Bar: proof before pitch

**What it is:** A slim horizontal trust strip directly under the hero — certification and partnership marks with no sales copy.

**Why it's there:** B2B and institutional buyers in Saudi Arabia scan for compliance signals before reading anything else. Placing them immediately after the hero converts the emotional impression into rational permission to keep scrolling. This is deliberately *before* any product content.

**Content (exactly these, nothing invented):** ISO 9001 · ISO 14001 · ISO 45001 · WRAS Approved · SASO Certified · National Water Company Partner · Licensed Engineering Firm · Saudi Vision 2030 Partner.

**Draft micro-headline (small, above the marks):**
> Certified. Approved. Accountable.

**Layout/composition:** White band on the navy hero's tail, logos/marks in a single row (wrapping to two rows on tablet, a compact 2×4 grid on mobile). Monochrome or navy-tinted logo treatment so the strip reads as one calm unit rather than a cluttered logo soup. Each mark can be lightly annotated on hover/tap with one line (e.g., "ISO 45001 — Occupational Health & Safety"). No links out; this strip is evidence, not navigation.

## Section 3 — Who We Are: the numbers band

**What it is:** A short positioning statement paired with three or four hard numbers.

**Why it's there:** After proof marks, the buyer wants scale and substance in ten seconds. Numbers do this faster than paragraphs. It also creates the page's first navy "breather" section, establishing the alternating white/navy rhythm.

**Draft headline:**
> **The Kingdom's irrigation infrastructure partner.**

**Supporting copy:**
> Demas Group is a licensed engineering firm headquartered in Riyadh, supplying and supporting irrigation, landscape, and water-management systems for commercial, municipal, and industrial projects across Saudi Arabia. A partner of the National Water Company and a contributor to Vision 2030's green transformation.

**Stat set (use only verifiable figures):**
> **45+ years** in irrigation & landscape engineering · **~700 products** across five specialist categories · **Kingdom-wide** supply and support from Riyadh · **3 ISO certifications** (9001 / 14001 / 45001)

**Layout/composition:** Deep navy background. Copy block left (serif headline in white, gold underline accent), stat row right or beneath — large gold serif numerals with small white sans-serif labels. Generous vertical padding; this section should feel like a held breath, not a billboard.

## Section 4 — Product Categories: the catalog gateway

**What it is:** Five photography-led category cards linking into the WooCommerce catalog.

**Why it's there:** This is the commercial engine of the page — the shortest path from homepage to product. SKU counts per category are shown deliberately: for a B2B buyer, "267 fog system products" is itself a trust signal (depth of stock = reliability of supply).

**Draft headline:**
> **Five specialisms. One supplier.**

**Supporting intro copy:**
> Around 700 professional-grade products — pipes, fittings, filtration, valves, controllers, drippers, rotors, valve boxes, and fog systems — specified for commercial performance and Saudi conditions.

**The five cards (name · count · one-line descriptor):**
> **Fog Systems** — 267 products — *High-pressure misting and cooling for outdoor commercial spaces.*
> **Landscape** — 166 products — *Equipment and materials for large-scale landscape engineering.*
> **Irrigation** — 122 products — *Drip, spray, and rotor systems with filtration and control.*
> **Swimming Pool** — 59 products — *Circulation, filtration, and finishing equipment.*
> **Industrial Tool Services** — 53 products — *Professional tools and servicing for site teams.*

**Layout/composition:** White background. A five-card asymmetric grid: the two largest categories (Fog Systems, Landscape) as wide feature cards on the top row, the remaining three as equal cards below. Each card: full-bleed photograph with a navy gradient from the bottom, category name in serif, product count as a small gold badge, one-line descriptor, and an "arrow" affordance. Whole card is the link. On mobile, cards stack full-width in the order above. Each card maps cleanly to a WooCommerce product category archive — no custom landing pages required at launch.

## Section 5 — How We Work: the engineering process

**What it is:** A four-step process section showing the company operates as an engineering partner, not a parts counter.

**Why it's there:** This is the key differentiator against pure distributors. Institutional buyers procure *outcomes*; showing a disciplined process de-risks the purchase and justifies premium positioning. It also gives the "licensed engineering firm" claim somewhere concrete to live.

**Draft headline:**
> **From site survey to sustained performance.**

**Supporting intro copy:**
> Every project moves through the same disciplined sequence — the same rigor behind our ISO 9001 certification.

**The four steps:**
> **01 · Assess** — *We start on your site, not in a catalog: water sources, pressures, soil, climate, and the demands of your project.*
> **02 · Engineer** — *Our licensed engineers design and specify the complete system — hydraulics, zoning, filtration, and control.*
> **03 · Supply** — *We deliver from deep Kingdom-wide stock, with every component matched to the approved specification.*
> **04 · Support** — *Commissioning, maintenance, and technical support keep systems performing for their full design life.*

**Layout/composition:** Light warm-neutral background (subtle sand tone from the photography palette) to break the white/navy alternation. Steps arranged horizontally as four columns connected by a thin gold line — a quiet visual echo of a pipeline. Oversized serif step numerals in pale navy behind each column. On mobile, the line runs vertically down the left with steps stacked. Optional closing line beneath the steps: *"One accountable partner across the entire lifecycle."*

## Section 6 — Closing CTA: start the conversation

**What it is:** The final conversion section — a direct invitation to request a quotation or consult an engineer.

**Why it's there:** B2B purchases here start with a conversation, not a cart. The page's last impression should make that conversation feel easy, fast, and expert-led.

**Draft headline:**
> **Let's put your project on solid ground.**

**Supporting copy:**
> Send us your drawings, your BOQ, or just the problem you're trying to solve. Our engineering team responds with a specification and quotation — typically within two business days.

**CTAs:** Primary — *Request a Quotation* (gold). Secondary — *Call Our Riyadh Office* (click-to-dial on mobile). Consider a WhatsApp Business contact as a third quiet option — standard practice for Saudi B2B.

**Layout/composition:** Return to deep navy for a bookended feel with the hero. Use frame 04 of the hero sequence (the thriving finished landscape) as a darkened background image — closing the visual story where it ends. Headline centered, CTAs side by side beneath, and a slim contact line under them (Riyadh address, phone, email). This flows directly into the site footer.

## Implementation notes (non-visual)

**Native blocks mapping.** Every section above decomposes into standard WordPress primitives: Cover blocks (hero, closing CTA), Columns/Group blocks (credentials bar, stats, process), and a Query Loop or hand-built card grid for categories. Nothing here assumes a page builder; the asymmetric category grid is the only piece needing custom block styles.

**Bilingual readiness.** Even if launch is English-only, compose every section to survive RTL mirroring for a future Arabic version: avoid text baked into images, keep captions as live text, and design the process line and card grid so they mirror cleanly.

**Photography discipline.** Outside the hero's generated sequence, all imagery should be real project/product photography in the same golden-hour, infrastructure-first register. One inconsistent stock image undermines the entire premium repositioning.

**Claims hygiene — resolved.** Experience confirmed at 45 years. All certifications shown on the live site are valid. Permission confirmed to reference National Water Company and Vision 2030 partnerships by name as written above.
